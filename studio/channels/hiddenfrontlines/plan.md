## Rules
- Series: "Weird War Tech" – one strange real WW2-era invention per short. Chapter = invention name; title ends with "#shorts" (series number in description: "Weird War Tech #N").
- Hook (line 1, < 2.5 s): the absurd fact as a flat statement: "In 1943, Britain tried to build an aircraft carrier out of ice." First frame = the blueprint of the object, full screen, already drawn (draw-on details may follow).
- Structure: absurd claim → how it was supposed to work (blueprint callouts) → the test → what killed it / what happened → stamp ("CANCELLED", "CLASSIFIED", "IT WORKED").
- Length 25–40 s, max ~85 words. Title pattern: "[Country/Army] [did absurd thing]. [Short payoff]." e.g. "The US Army Fought With Rubber Tanks. It Worked."
- Style: blueprints + declassified file cards + red stamps. Objects large and centred, labelled callouts (cards).
- Every fact verified with 2 sources (dates, places, numbers).
