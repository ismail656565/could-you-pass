## Rules
- Format: one myth per short, 30–40 s, max ~85 words. Structure: myth (painted in its culture's style) → "then someone dug" → the find in the field notebook (codex) → what is proven / what is still debated (one line).
- Hook (line 1, < 2.5 s): the myth stated as a dare – "Everyone said X was a myth." / "For 2,000 years nobody believed …". First frame = the most dramatic painted plate, never a title card.
- On-screen: a card at 0 s with the myth in ≤4 words ("THE LABYRINTH?"), a stamp on the reveal ("FOUND 1900", "REAL", "DEBATED").
- Title pattern (exploit): "Everyone Said [X] Was a Myth. Then Someone Dug. #shorts" style – two short sentences, setup + payoff. Vary the wording, never reuse exactly.
- Numbers win: put a concrete number in title or hook when the facts give one (years, soldiers, metres).
- Styles: culture style for the myth plate (blackfigure, mosaic, tomb, codex, ukiyoe…) + codex notebook for the dig. Map plates with arrows (codex) are allowed and encouraged (competitor pattern).
- No series number needed in titles; chapter = place name.
