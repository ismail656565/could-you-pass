## Rules
- Topic filter (in this order): 1) affects the viewer's apps/money/devices/AI/internet (streaming, phones, social apps, games, prices, AI tools); 2) a big tech/space story with a twist; 3) a court case against a big company. SKIP: B2B mergers, sports rulings, stock moves. ≥ 2 reliable outlets, < 72 h old.
- Title EXPERIMENT (all news shorts from now): curiosity + consequence, not the headline. Patterns: "[Company] Just Did Something Nobody Expected…", "Why [Thing You Use] Is About to Change…", "[Subject] Goes Deeper Than You Think". Keep one concrete noun (brand/app) in the title; no invented claims; ≤ 60 chars.
- Hook (< 2.5 s) = consequence for YOU ("Your playlists are about to shrink."), line 2 = the number; reveal by 12 s; last line "Proven: … Debated: …".
- First frame = red "BREAKING" bar card on top + hook card ≤ 4 words in a white box (competitor look, own design), strongest plate behind. Never a title card.
- Length 30–40 s, max ~85 words, 5 lines. Styles: newscollage, blueprint, letterpress, notebook, bauhaus, ballpoint. Stamps PROVEN / DEBATED / ALLEGED.
- Follow-ups: when a covered story moves (Nvidia, Huawei sentencing, SpaceX, Tidal deletions start), make it 1 of 2 daily shorts; description "Update #N".
- Longs: 9–15 min, title "The [Subject] Story Goes Deeper Than You Think" style; meta.json "thumb" 2–4 huge words ≠ title ("WHO KNEW?", "11 DAYS OF CHAOS"), hl = shock word, shot = strongest object plate; never an accusation as fact.
- Real people: generic figures or text only, never a likeness. "alleged"/"reportedly" for anything unproven.
