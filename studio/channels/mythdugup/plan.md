## Rules
- Topic: one story from the last 24–72 h with a twist or a big number (tech, business, science, space, world, courts against companies/institutions). Must be confirmed by ≥ 2 reliable outlets.
- Format: 30–40 s, max ~85 words, 5 lines. Line 1 (< 2.5 s) = the shocking fact; line 2 = escalation with a number; reveal by 12 s; last line = "Proven: … Debated: …" in one sentence.
- First frame = strongest plate + hook card (≤ 4 words), never a title card.
- Title: curiosity + names, e.g. "Nvidia Fired Its CEO. 11 Days Later He Was Back. #shorts".
- Styles: newscollage, blueprint, letterpress, notebook, bauhaus, ballpoint (no culture-of-origin rule anymore). Stamps PROVEN / DEBATED / ALLEGED.
- Follow-ups: prefer 1 of 2 daily shorts as an update of a story we already covered, if there is a real new development (court ruling, deal approved/blocked, launch result). Title "[Subject] Just [New Development]. #shorts"; description "Update #N".
- Long thumbnails (meta.json "thumb"): 2–4 huge words ≠ title, question or shock word, never an accusation as fact ("11 DAYS OF CHAOS", "WHO KNEW?"), hl = the shock word, shot = the strongest object/plate (no likeness). Add a red "BREAKING" style stamp in the shot plate if possible.
- Real people: generic figures or text only, never a likeness. Use "alleged"/"reportedly" for anything unproven.
