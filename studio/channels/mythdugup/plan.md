## Rules
- Format: one myth per short, 30–40 s, max ~85 words. Myth (culture-style plate) → "then someone dug" → find (codex) → proven vs. debated (one line).
- Hook (line 1, < 2.5 s): the myth as a dare – "Everyone said X was a myth." First frame = most dramatic painted plate, never a title card.
- NEW line-2 rule (retention): line 2 (≈ 2.5–6 s) = escalation with a concrete number or the first proof ("Then a shovel hit a wall. Nine cities deep."). No dates, no names, no background in lines 1–2. Change plate/camera move at ~4.5 s.
- Reveal (the find) before second 12.
- Card at 0 s with the myth in ≤4 words; stamp on reveal ("FOUND 1900", "REAL", "DEBATED").
- Title pattern (exploit): "Everyone Said [X] Was a Myth. Then Someone Dug. #shorts" style – setup + payoff, vary wording. Number in title/hook when facts give one.
- Styles: culture style for myth plate + codex notebook for the dig; map plates with arrows encouraged.
