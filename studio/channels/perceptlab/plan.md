## Rules
- Every short is a TEST the viewer does: line 1 is an instruction or a question to "you" (stare / close one eye / pick A or B). Never open with a fact or a name.
- First frame: the illusion itself, full screen, plus a question card ("A or B?", "Which is bigger?") at 0 s. No intro, no title card.
- Length 20–35 s, max ~70 words. Reveal at ~60 % of the runtime, stamp "YOUR BRAIN: FOOLED" (or similar) on the reveal, last line loops back ("Now look again.").
- Title pattern: "[Instruction or question] + [promise] + emoji #shorts", e.g. "Which Circle Is Bigger? Your Brain Picks Wrong 🧠 #shorts". Use "Brain Test #N" in the title for quiz-type episodes (series).
- Style: chalkboard / lab drawings; high contrast, the tested object large and centred.
- Facts: name the illusion + year in the last line (true and verified).
